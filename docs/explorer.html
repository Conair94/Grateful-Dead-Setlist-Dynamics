ℹ️ About This Page

Browse any of the 1,968 Grateful Dead shows with known setlists (1965–1995) and view the show's mood arc — the trajectory of energy, tempo, danceability, brightness, and dynamic range across the setlist.

Audio features were extracted with Essentia from live recordings, era-normalized to remove recording-quality bias, and plotted as catalog percentiles: 0.9 means the song is in the top 10% of the catalog for that feature.

Use Compare to overlay a second show (dashed lines), and Download CSV to take the data with you.
